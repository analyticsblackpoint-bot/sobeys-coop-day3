3 · Two brains, same prompt

Rule brain

Keywords → fixed answers from the table. Cannot invent. Anything it does not recognise gets the fallback line.

(score a prompt first)

LLM (your chatbot)

Predicts the next words from your prompt. Will answer anything, confidently, including things that are not true, unless your prompt gives it the facts and tells it what to do when it doesn't know.

The trap only stops working when the facts are in the prompt and the prompt says what to do when they aren't. That line is the fallback line, and it's in every skill today.
