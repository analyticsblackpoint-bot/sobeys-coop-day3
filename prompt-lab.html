LLM: predicts the next word, again and again

A real (tiny) next-word model, trained right now on the … lines of Snack Bar text on this page. A real LLM does exactly this with a trillion words and billions of learned numbers, which is why it sounds right even when it's wrong.

The rule brain only knows what someone typed into it. The LLM will finish any sentence. So: put the facts in the prompt, and tell it what to say when the facts run out. That's the fallback line.

When to use which skill

Each skill is a box you paste into any free chatbot. Use the one that matches what's missing from your prompt's score.
Score low on Context? → Fact Finder. You don't have the facts yet (prices, hours, what's low, what sold).Example: "I want the bot to answer 'what's on clearance?' but I only have a guess." → paste the box, get a table of facts with links. The red-teamer opens every link.
Score low on Role or Format? → System Prompt Writer. The assistant doesn't know who it's talking to or how to answer.Example: "It writes essays when a shopper wants one line." → paste the box with your best prompt. You get an instruction sheet with ROLE, AUDIENCE, FORMAT and a FALLBACK LINE.
Prompt scores 8+? → Bot Builder. Turn the instruction sheet into one HTML page that works offline.Example: "We have the facts and the prompt; now we need the page with the chat and the chart." → paste the box. Save the reply as .html and double-click it.
Score low on Check, or the bot answered the trap? → Red-Team Tester. Five questions that try to make it invent.Example: "Someone asked 'is there a student discount?' and it said yes." → run the five tests, add the missing refuse word, run again.
Page works? → Pitch Coach. A 3-minute outline where everyone speaks and the live demo includes the question the bot refuses.Example: "We have 3 minutes and four people." → paste the box with your results; get six parts that add up to 180 seconds.

Scoring 8 or more means the prompt is ready to become your bot's instruction sheet. Keep the best one.

The preview answers from 8 to 10 rules per assistant, the same way the Bot Builder page will. Count the keyword hits, pick the best rule, show the source. No rule matched, or a refuse word in the message, means the fallback line. An email or a phone number means the privacy warning, before anything else.
